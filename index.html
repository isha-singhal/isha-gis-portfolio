Projects

Automated Building Extraction from Satellite Images

2024 - 2025
M.Tech Thesis | Computer Vision

Evaluated deep learning and generative models (Modified U-Net, DeepLabv3+, and Pix2Pix cGAN) for the task of extracting clear building footprints from high-resolution, multispectral satellite imagery.

  • Curated a robust training dataset of 8,716 high-resolution, multiscale image-label pairs by consolidating four global benchmark datasets.
  • Developed a dynamic ResNet34-backed U-Net using cyclical learning rates and a hybrid Combo Loss (BCE + Dice), achieving a 94.18% validation accuracy and 0.79 F1-score.
  • Tested model resilience on unseen imagery across distinct settings: Dehradun (heterogeneous urban area) & Chandigarh (planned urban area). Established critical cross-domain trade-offs between stable CNN segmentation consistency and visually continuous GAN boundary generation.

Water Quality Monitoring in Majuli, Assam Using Google Earth Engine

2025 - Present
Spatial Analytics

Developed a cloud-based remote sensing workflow in Google Earth Engine (GEE) to monitor water turbidity across the Majuli district, Assam, using Sentinel-2 satellite imagery (2019–2023).

  • Applied NDWI (Normalized Difference Water Index) and NDTI (Normalized Difference Turbidity Index) to extract water bodies and assess spatial and temporal variations in water quality.
  • Performed annual, monthly, and time-series analysis to identify seasonal turbidity trends, revealing increased turbidity during monsoon periods due to rainfall, flooding, and sediment transport.
  • Generated interactive maps, turbidity classification layers, and visualization dashboards to support environmental monitoring and water resource management decisions in ecologically sensitive riverine regions.
